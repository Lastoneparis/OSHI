Our manifesto

Privacy isn't a feature. It's the product.

Every other messenger you've used logs your friends, harvests your contact graph, and stores your messages on someone else's server in a country you've never visited. They call it security; we call it surveillance with a nice icon.

OSHI is different by design. There is no central server holding your conversations. No phone number tying your identity to a SIM card. No cloud backups leaking your photos. Your messages are end-to-end encrypted with X25519 + ChaCha20-Poly1305 and never decrypted anywhere but on your device.

When the internet works, we use it — but invisibly, with steganographic covert channels that look like ordinary HTTPS. When it doesn't, OSHI speaks Bluetooth LE and Wi-Fi Direct mesh, hop-routes through nearby devices, and keeps you connected through blackouts, festivals, deserts, and demonstrations.

Open source. Zero metadata. No telemetry. No ads. The only entity that knows what you said is you.

What's inside

Everything you'd expect from a messenger. And the part nobody else built.

Bluetooth + Wi-Fi mesh

When there's no cell signal and no internet, OSHI keeps working. Messages hop-route through nearby devices, encrypted end-to-end at every leg, so you stay in touch through blackouts, festivals, and natural disasters.

  • Multi-hop relay (TTL 7)
  • Encrypted at every hop
  • Reaches ~300 m per hop in dense networks
  • Battery-aware: ~3 % per hour idle

Zero metadata

No phone number, no email, no friend graph stored anywhere. Identity is a public key pair generated on your device.

Steganographic covert channels

6 ways to pass messages through inspection: HTTPS look-alike, audio modem, image LSB, DNS, NTP, ICMP.

Offline maps for 180+ regions

Download a country once and navigate it forever. Tiles, road graph, addresses, and POIs — all stored on-device. Turn-by-turn routing without sending a byte to Google or Apple. Even searches like "12 rue de Rivoli" or "pharmacy" stay local.

On-device AI

Apple Intelligence + Gemini Nano locally. Smart replies, summarisation, translation — never your tokens, never your data.

Bot API

Build encrypted bots that talk over mesh. Webhook-compatible. No central server required.

Open source

Crypto audited, code on GitHub, builds reproducible. Verify everything we say with the source.

Security in depth

You don't have to trust us. That's the point.

01

Identity is a key, not an account

Your handle is the SHA-256 fingerprint of a public key generated on first launch. Nothing to forget, nothing to phish, nothing to legally compel from us — we never had it.

02

X25519 + ChaCha20-Poly1305

Industry-standard end-to-end encryption. Double-ratchet forward secrecy on every conversation. No backdoor, no key-escrow, no plaintext shadow copy on our infrastructure.

03

Zero-knowledge by construction

OSHI's servers see encrypted blobs and routing hints — never plaintext, never the recipient's name. The minimum architecture for the maximum privacy.

04

Offline by design

The decryption keys, the maps, the AI inference — everything that touches your data runs on your hardware. The cloud is optional, not assumed.
